AlgoMind Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

1. Overview

AlgoMind ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how the AlgoMind Chrome Extension and Web Dashboard collect, use, and store information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

AlgoMind collects minimal data necessary to provide spaced repetition DSA tracking:

  • Authentication Information: Secure tokens used to authenticate your AlgoMind dashboard account.
  • Problem Solves Metadata: Problem titles, difficulty levels, and platform names (LeetCode, GeeksforGeeks, Codeforces) detected upon accepted code submission.

3. Single Purpose Compliance

The single purpose of AlgoMind is to track Data Structures & Algorithms problem solves across competitive coding sites and schedule spaced repetition revision reminders.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties or advertising networks.
  • We do NOT use data for lending or creditworthiness determinations.
  • All data is transferred securely over HTTPS.
